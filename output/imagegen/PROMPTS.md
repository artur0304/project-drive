# First editing trial

Tool: built-in imagegen. Four independent requests; no multi-provider comparison performed.

Inputs: photos 2–5 are edit targets; photo 1 is the wheel reference for each request.

## Prompt used for all four requests

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the FIRST input photograph, which is the target car photo. SECOND input is ONLY the wheel design reference. Produce one edited photograph, same portrait framing and full scene as target, no collage. Make exactly three changes to the main foreground car: (1) replace all its visible wheel rims with the exact black and diamond-cut silver angular multi-spoke BMW rim design in the reference, preserving spoke geometry and BMW center caps even on Range Rover, with correct perspective, realistic tires, original wheel centers, overall tire diameter and ride height; (2) remove dark window tint so windows are realistically clear automotive glass with subtle natural tint, preserve reflections appropriate to original lighting, cabin visibility plausible and no invented conspicuous occupants; (3) wrap only painted body panels in deep emerald green glossy vinyl, consistent hue across the series, preserving original lighting and reflections. Preserve car model, body shape, trim, grilles, badges, lights, plate text, geometry, camera, background, people, signs, road, weather, and other vehicles unchanged. Keep original nighttime/daytime exposure. Do not brighten the whole scene. Do not restyle the car, alter its body kit or repaint black plastic/chrome. Accuracy to the source and exact reference wheel design over beauty.

Additional instruction for photo 3: The target is the black BMW sedan in the foreground; do NOT modify the white SUV beside it.

## Visual review

All four outputs show green bodywork, lighter windows and replacement wheels. Wheel spoke geometry does not exactly match the supplied reference. These results must not be recorded as successful exact wheel replacements. Interior details revealed through glass are generated, not recovered from the originals. This trial does not establish a quality acceptance rate or a winning provider.
