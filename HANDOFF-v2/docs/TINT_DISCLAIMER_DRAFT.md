# Window tint disclaimer (draft)

> **Draft; legal review required for every launch country.**

Project Drive shows an approximate visual preview. The displayed darkness is not
a measurement of visible light transmission (VLT), and screen brightness, weather,
camera exposure and AI processing can make it look different from installed film.

Tint laws differ by country, region, window and vehicle type. Some places prohibit
or tightly limit windshield and front-side tint. Before installation, the customer
must confirm local requirements and ask a qualified installer to measure the final
VLT. Project Drive does not certify that a selected tint is road legal.

Suggested short UI text:

> Visual preview only. Tint limits vary by location and window. Confirm legal VLT
> with a qualified installer before installation.
