## Service

Project Drive creates AI-generated previews of vehicle wraps, window tint and
wheel changes from photographs submitted by users. Results are visual concepts.
They are not engineering drawings, fitment guarantees, colour proofs or legal advice.

## User content

You must have the right to upload each photograph and wheel reference. You grant
Project Drive a limited permission to store and process that content only to
operate the service, generate requested previews, prevent abuse and provide support.
You retain ownership of your content.

Do not upload unlawful content, personal documents, photographs that expose
unrelated people without permission, or wheel imagery that you are not allowed to use.

## AI results

AI can change details unintentionally, including vehicle geometry, reflections,
logos, wheel design, window boundaries and background objects. Inspect the result
before relying on it. A preview does not promise that a physical modification is
available, safe, compatible or identical in colour and finish.

## Credits during beta

Beta credits are test units for AI passes. They have no cash value and cannot be
transferred. If a generation fails technically, the service is designed to return
the reserved credit automatically. Complaints about visual quality may require
manual review. Paid credit sales remain disabled during the closed beta.

## Availability and accounts

The beta may change, pause or end. Keep account credentials private. We may limit
or suspend accounts used for abuse, automated attacks or infringement.
